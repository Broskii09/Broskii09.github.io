About Ray Leo’s

A live music venue, full bar, and kitchen on Franklin Street in Evansville.

Lamasco

Music first. Bar and kitchen right behind it.

Ray Leo’s at Lamasco sits in Evansville’s historic Lamasco neighborhood with a focus on live music, show-night energy, and a room built for people who want food, drinks, and a reason to stay out.

The website direction is intentionally dark, direct, and photo-forward: less decorative diner clutter, more black venue grit, poster energy, and Franklin Street atmosphere.
