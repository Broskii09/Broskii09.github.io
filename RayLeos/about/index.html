About

Ray Leo’s at Lamasco.

A Franklin Street live music venue, bar, and kitchen built around loud rooms, local energy, and nights that run on more than background music.

Find us at 1331 W Franklin St in Evansville’s Lamasco neighborhood.

The room

Music first. Bar and kitchen close behind.

Ray Leo’s is a show room before anything else: a place for local bills, touring bands, and Franklin Street crowds to meet up around live music.

The full bar and kitchen support the night, whether you are here before doors, between sets, or after the last chord rings out.
