About

Ray Leo’s at Lamasco.

A room with a pulse: bands on the stage, food from the kitchen, drinks at the bar, and Franklin Street energy running through the walls.

Ray Leo’s sits at 1331 W Franklin St in Evansville’s Lamasco neighborhood.

The room

Music first. Everything else keeps it moving.

Ray Leo’s is built around the kind of nights that need a stage, a crowd, and a little volume. Local bills, touring bands, show-night regulars, and Franklin Street energy all belong in the room.

The name carries family roots. The place is about making new nights worth remembering.
