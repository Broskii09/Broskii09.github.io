About

Ray Leo’s at Lamasco.

A Franklin Street live music venue with a full bar, kitchen, and neighborhood energy.

Ray Leo’s brings shows, food, drinks, and late-night hangs together in Evansville’s Lamasco neighborhood.

The venue

Live music first.

The room is centered on bands, show nights, and the Franklin Street crowd, with the bar and kitchen rounding out the night.
