About

A room for original music on Franklin Street.

Ray Leo’s at Lamasco is not trying to be just another watering hole.

It is an all-ages live music venue, kitchen, and full-service bar in a Franklin Street room with real history in the walls.

The room

Detroy Grocery, Lamasco, and a room that survived.

Located on West Franklin Street, the building started as Detroy Grocery in 1894.

During Prohibition, the property allegedly saw its share of illegal gambling and bootleg alcohol running before receiving its first liquor license in 1934. The room has history. It has stories. It has survived enough to know what a good night sounds like.

What it is now

Today, Ray Leo’s is an all-ages live music venue, kitchen, and full-service bar operating in that legendary space.

Original local music, open mic nights, karaoke, trivia, touring acts, bar food, cocktails: all under one roof.

Why it exists

The goal is simple: give Evansville a creative room where people can play, gather, connect, and keep original music alive.

Music first. Food and drinks close by. All ages unless noted per event.

The names

Big Ray, Leo, and something new to become.

The name Ray Leo’s comes from Jeremiah’s grandfathers, Big Ray and Leo.

The place honors them, honors the history of Lamasco, and gives the room something new to become.
