About

A room for original music on Franklin Street.

Ray Leo’s at Lamasco is a live music venue, full bar, and kitchen built for loud nights, original bands, and the Lamasco history running through Franklin Street.

Find us at 1331 W Franklin St in Evansville’s Lamasco neighborhood.

The names

Big Ray, Leo, and a place of total love.

Ray Leo’s is named for Jeremiah’s grandfathers: Raymond Big Ray Hankammer and Leo.

Big Ray was a World War II paratrooper. The name honors family roots, but the room is aimed forward: a new spot for bands, friends, and nights that deserve to be remembered.

Franklin Street roots

The goal is to honor the long history of Lamasco while making something that belongs to right now.

Music first. Food and drinks close by. All ages unless noted per event.

Why the room exists

Jeremiah has been wrapped up in music since he was 14: playing, writing, performing, recording, mixing, and chasing shows around the region.

That early all-ages, original-music energy is the point here. The scene is not dead. For everyone still lifting it up and holding it down: hell yeah dude.

Original music

Another spot for bands to play.

Ray Leo’s exists to give original music another home in Evansville: a real stage, a real crowd, and room for local and touring acts to make noise.

Great stage, killer sound, and a bitchin lighting rig. That is the promise.
